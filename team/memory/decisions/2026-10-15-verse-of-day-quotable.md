# Ruling: Verse of the Day pool must be quotable verses (2026-10-15)

Founder, Oct 15, 2026: "It should make sure that the verses
aren't just filler verses. They should be ones that people
can quote."

- The Verse of the Day selection standard changes from pure
  cross-reference prominence to QUOTABILITY: verses that stand
  alone with complete sense and that people actually quote and
  memorize. Filler (genealogies, transitions, fragments) is
  excluded regardless of connection count.
- A revision package was dispatched on this ruling.
